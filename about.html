Who I am

I'm Benjamin Beres — someone who loves turning ideas into real products. I'm naturally curious, I enjoy problem-solving, and I'm happiest when I'm building something that feels clean, simple, and actually helpful to people.

I'm also the person people come to when something breaks. I like tracing an issue back to its root cause, fixing it, and then documenting it so it doesn't happen again. That mix of building and troubleshooting is what drew me toward information systems in the first place.

Outside of school and projects, I'm big on staying disciplined and improving over time — whether it's learning a new tool, optimizing a project, or sharpening my communication and teamwork. I grew up playing varsity golf and basketball, and that same competitiveness and routine carries over into how I approach my work today.

Education & Field 🎓

What I study & build

I studied Computer Information Systems at Kent State, which put me at the intersection of technology and business. I've built projects across web development, analytics dashboards, automation, and database work — with a growing focus on data analytics and decision-support tools.

My strengths are using Python and SQL to analyze data, building dashboards that explain insights clearly, and developing full-stack projects when needed (Flask, React, APIs). I also work comfortably in database and systems design, from ER diagrams and UML modeling to troubleshooting the systems that keep data flowing.
